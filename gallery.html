Visual Tour of the Museum

Explore these high-resolution images highlighting our exterior architecture, inner rooms, and archived manuscripts.

The first photo shows the interior of the museum. The room has traditional furniture and wooden elements. The atmosphere makes the museum look like a historical place and helps visitors understand the period connected with Saken Seifullin. The second photo shows personal belongings displayed in a museum showcase. There are different objects, including a clock, dishes and other personal items. These objects are interesting because they show some details of everyday life in the past. The third photo shows a suit displayed in a glass showcase. The suit is presented together with other historical objects. It gives visitors an opportunity to see a personal item connected with the historical period.
