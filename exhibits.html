Key Artifacts Catalog - museum exhibits

This section presents 8 significant exhibits from the museum's collection,reflecting the life, work and legacy of Saken Seifullin

Historical Exhibits of the Museum
Exhibit title Type Year Description
"Aisha" (first edition) Book 1927 A novel by Saken Seifullin, one of the first Kazakh works about the life of a women.
Death Certificate of S. Seifullin’s son, Ayan Document 1917 Official document confirming the death of his son Ayan.
Letter from the editorial office of “Qazaq Ädebieti” Document 1934 A letter from the editorial office regarding Seifullin’s literary activity.
Petition by Gulbakhram Seifullina Document 1936 A petition sent by Gulbakhram Seifullina in support of her husband.
Painting “Saken on the Path to Freedom” Painting 1980 A painting dedicated to Saken Seifullin’s life and struggle.
Manuscript of a poem Manuscript 1922 An original manuscript of one of Seifullin’s poems.
Photograph of Saken Seifullin Photograph 1910s A rare photograph of the writer from the early years.
Personal document (membership card) Document 1929 A membership card of the Writers’ Union of the USSR.
