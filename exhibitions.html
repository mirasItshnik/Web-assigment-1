Permanent Halls

The museum consists of six thematic halls representing different eras of Seifullin's life:

  • Hall 1: Childhood and Youth in the Steppe
  • Hall 2: Educational Years in Omsk
  • Hall 3: Pedagogical and Public Activities
  • Hall 4: Literary Masterpieces and Publications
  • Hall 5: Personal Belongings and Office Re-creation
  • Hall 6: Memory and Contemporary Heritage

Temporary Exhibition: Seifullin and Music

This special display highlights Seifullin's passion for music, featuring traditional musical instruments and original sheet music notes transcribed by the author himself.

Dates: September 1, 2026 – December 1, 2026.
