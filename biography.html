Saken Seifullin (1894–1938)

Poet, writer, educator, and state leader of Kazakhstan.

Life Journey

1894: Born in Akmola region. Developed a deep passion for Kazakh folklore and poetry from early childhood.

1913–1916: Studied at the Omsk Teachers' Seminary, actively participating in public youth movements.

1938: Tragically executed during political repressions and posthumously rehabilitated as a national hero.
