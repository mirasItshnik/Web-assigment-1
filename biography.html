Early Life and Education

Saken Seifullin was born on October 15, 1894, in the Akmola region. From a young age, he demonstrated a profound interest in oral folklore and poetry.

He studied at the Omsk Teachers' Seminary, where he became actively involved in social movements and public education.

Literary Legacy

Seifullin played a crucial role in developing modern Kazakh prose and poetry. His notable works include:

  1. Unpromising Way (Thorny Path) — an autobiographical novel detailing revolutionary events.
  2. Kokshe-Tau — a famous poem celebrating the beauty of Kazakh nature.
  3. Red Falcon — a groundbreaking drama in Kazakh literature.

Tragic End and Rehabilitation

In 1937, Saken Seifullin fell victim to political repression and was executed in 1938. He was later posthumously rehabilitated, and his works remain a cornerstone of national cultural heritage.
