The Wall of Words

Saken Seifullin was a poet before he was anything else. This wall gathers lines from his verse and prose — each card a fragment of the voice that shaped modern Kazakh literature.

All texts are displayed for educational purposes as part of the museum's literary collection. Visit the Collection page to see the original manuscripts.
