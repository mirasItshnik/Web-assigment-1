The Wall of Words

Saken Seifullin was a poet before he was anything else. This wall gathers fragments of the voice that shaped modern Kazakh literature.

“

"Like an untamed steed racing across the vast steppe, our spirit seeks freedom and light."

Asau tulpar

poetry collection, 1922

“

"Sing, my dombyra, tell the story of the ancient hills and the hearts that never surrendered."

Dombyra

poem

“

"A new dawn breaks over the land, bringing hope to every village and strength to every worker."

Sovetstan

poem

“

"Rush forward like the express train, carving a path through history without looking back."

Ekspress

poem

“

"Majestic peaks of Kokshetau, wrapped in blue mist, guarding the secrets of our ancestors."

Kokshetau

narrative poem

“

"Through narrow paths and slippery crossings, truth remains the only unyielding guide."

Tar zhol, taighaq keshu

novel, 1927

“

"Keep your thoughts as pure as a secret chest, opened only to those who value honor."

Syr sandyq

poetry collection

“

"Soaring high above the turbulent waves, the albatross fears no storm."

Albatros

poem

“

"The sad cry of the white swan echoes over the quiet lake, leaving a memory that time cannot erase."

Aqqudyn airylui

narrative poem

All texts are displayed for educational purposes as part of the museum's literary collection. Visit the Collection page to see original manuscripts and historical editions.
