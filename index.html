Welcome to Saken Seifullin Museum

Discover the heritage of Kazakhstan's premier writer, poet, and statesman in the historic wooden landmark of Astana.

Preserving Cultural History

The Saken Seifullin State Museum is dedicated to preserving and presenting the life, literary works, and political achievements of Saken Seifullin (1894–1938).

Housed in a 19th-century wooden architectural monument, the museum offers a unique journey through early 20th-century Kazakh history, showcasing original manuscripts, personal artifacts, and rare photographs.

Museum Highlights

Personal Belongings

Explore unique personal effects, including Seifullin’s original suit, desk accessories, and handwritten notes.

Literary Legacy

First edition printings of famous poems, literary journals, and historic documents from the 1920s.

Archival Collection

Over 5,000 preserved items illustrating the cultural awakening and history of Kazakhstan.
