Museum History

Discover the rich history, founding milestones, and cultural legacy of the State Museum named after Saken Seifullin.

History of the Museum

The State Museum named after Saken Seifullin was established in 1984 in Astana. The museum was created to preserve the memory of the great Kazakh writer, statesman, and public figure Saken Seifullin, introducing his life and work to future generations.

Over the years, the museum has become an important cultural and educational center, where visitors can learn about the life of S. Seifullin, the history of Kazakhstan, and the development of the national literary heritage.

1984 Official Opening

Establishment of the State Museum

On July 9, 1984, the museum officially opened its doors in a historical wooden merchant house built in the late 19th century in Akmolinsk.

Building Architecture & Legacy

The museum building itself is an architectural monument of the late 19th century, preserved in its original wooden style with intricate Kazakh decorative elements.

Historical Fact
In this historic building, Saken Seifullin once worked in the 1920s, making it not only a museum storehouse but an authentic living monument of history.
