Museum History

The story of the museum, its building and its place in Kazakhstan's cultural heritage.

History of the Museum

The State Museum named after Saken Seifullin was established in 1984 in Astana. The museum was created to preserve the memory of the great Kazakh writer, statesman and public figure Saken Seifullin, and to introduce his life and work to future generations. Over the years, the museum has become an important cultural and educational center, where visitors can learn about the life of S. Seifullin, the history of Kazakhstan and the development of the national literary heritage.

Opening of the Museum

The museum officially opened its doors in 1984. Since then, it has been continuously developing, expanding its collections and improving its exhibitions. Today, it attracts students, researchers, artists and tourists who want to learn more about Saken Seifullin and his contribution to Kazakh culture and literature.
